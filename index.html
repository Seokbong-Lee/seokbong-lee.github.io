About Me

I am an Assistant Professor of Coastal Engineering at Salisbury University. My work investigates the physical processes governing sediment transport, bedform development, and coastal morphology under waves and currents.

Research Interests

  • Coastal sediment transport
  • Wave–current interactions
  • Seafloor ripples and bedforms
  • Coastal morphodynamics
  • Numerical and data-driven modeling
