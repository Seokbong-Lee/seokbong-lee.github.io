About Me

I am an Assistant Professor of Coastal Engineering in the Department of Physics and Engineering at Salisbury University. My research focuses on the physical processes governing sediment transport, seafloor bedform development, and coastal morphological change under waves and currents. I am particularly interested in understanding how sand ripples and larger bedforms form, evolve, and influence sediment transport in coastal and nearshore environments.

My work combines laboratory experiments, field observations, numerical modeling, and data-driven analysis. Through this integrated approach, I aim to improve our understanding and prediction of coastal processes across a range of spatial and temporal scales. I am also committed to involving undergraduate students in hands-on research and helping them develop practical skills in coastal engineering, experimental methods, scientific computing, and environmental data analysis.
